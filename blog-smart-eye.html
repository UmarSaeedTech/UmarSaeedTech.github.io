Building Real-Time Autonomous Surveillance: Firearm & Threat Detection with YOLOv12

Published by Umar Saeed • Computer Vision & Real-Time AI Specialist

1. Executive Summary

Automated threat monitoring requires high recall and sub-second detection latency across concurrent CCTV channels. The Smart Eye system integrates custom-trained YOLO architectures with OpenCV processing pipelines to detect firearms and industrial hazards in real time.

2. Technical Architecture & Engineering

  • Dataset Engineering: Curated and annotated specialized datasets with multi-angle camera perspectives.
  • Inference Optimization: Applied TensorRT and frame-skipping logic to preserve smooth frame rates during active detection.
  • Alert Pipeline: Integrated immediate spatial bounding-box renders alongside web hook alerts upon threat confidence crossing designated thresholds.

3. Deployment Considerations

Ensuring operational reliability meant balancing frame resolution against computational load on edge hardware. Future iterations incorporate digital twin representations for active spatial positioning inside monitored facilities.
